# Seeing the pages

`view_page` shows you a page exactly as members will see it, and `view_photo` shows you an uploaded photo. Use them to judge what the text view can't tell you:

- **Before placing photos**, look at the ones you're choosing between, so each goes beside the story it belongs to. Write captions and alt text only from what you can actually see and what the text says.
- **Photos the author attaches** come with their message as "Attached N photos: <ids>". They are already uploaded to the issue and listed with the unplaced photos. Look at each before placing it. Write its alt text from what it shows, and a caption only when the author's text supports one. If you leave any unplaced, say which and why.
- **When you've finished editing**, look at the pages you changed (always the cover, if you worked on it) and fix what reads badly: a heading stranded at the foot of a page, a sliver of text squeezed beside a photo, two photos crowding each other, a page that looks cramped or half empty, cover text that is hard to read against the photo or covers its subject.
- Views are limited per request, so don't spend them on pages you haven't touched, and don't look again unless you changed something.
